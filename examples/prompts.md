# Example prompts

Use these prompts with an MCP-compatible AI assistant connected to Snoooz.

## Setup

```text
List my Snoooz workspaces and help me choose the right one.

Inspect my workspace AI readiness and tell me what I should fix first.

Check whether Snoooz is fully enabled for my mailbox.
```

## Rules

```text
List my Snoooz rules in plain English.

Create a rule that drafts AI replies to customer support questions, but does not send them automatically.

Create a rule that sends AI replies to sales leads and uses a friendly, concise tone.

Create a rule that forwards urgent billing emails to finance@example.com.

Pause all rules in this workspace.

Find rules that may produce generic AI drafts.
```

## Follow-ups

```text
Configure my sales lead rule to send follow-ups every 3 days, up to 2 times.

Make this rule draft follow-ups instead of sending them automatically.

Disable follow-ups for all rules that send emails automatically.

Add follow-up instructions: keep it short, ask if they had time to review, and offer to schedule a call.
```

## Previewing

```text
Simulate which rule would match this email:
[Paste email here]

Preview the reply Snoooz would generate for this email:
[Paste email here]

Explain why Snoooz would or would not reply to this email.
```

## Training

```text
Train Snoooz from this FAQ:
Q: What is your refund policy?
A: ...

Search existing training articles about refunds.

Update the training article about onboarding with this improved answer.

Train Snoooz from this website URL.
```

## Troubleshooting

```text
Check why Snoooz is not replying from my mailbox.

Check mailbox authentication and subscription status.

Explain whether this mailbox is connected and operational.
```
